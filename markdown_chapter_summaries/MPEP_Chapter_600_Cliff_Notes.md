    Office will send notices, letters, and other communications relating
    to an application.
    -   **Plain English:** The practical rule is: an application must
        specify a correspondence address to which the Office will send
        notices, letters, and other communications relating to an
        application.

    correspondence address.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: the Office should be
        promptly notified of any change in correspondence address.
-   See MPEP § 711.03(c) for treatment of petitions to revive
    applications abandoned as a consequence of failure to timely receive
    an Office action at the correspondence address of record (e.g.,
    because the Office action was mailed to the incorrect correspondence
    address).
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: see MPEP § 711.03(c) for
        treatment of petitions to revive applications abandoned as a
        consequence of failure to timely receive an Office action at the
        correspondence address of record (e.g., because the Office
        action was mailed to the incorrect correspondence address).

    associated with a Customer Number, a separate notification must be

## § 609.06 --- Information Printed on Patent

-   A citation listed on form PTO/SB/08 and considered by the examiner
    will be printed on the patent.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: a citation listed on form
        PTO/SB/08 and considered by the examiner will be printed on the
        patent.
-   A citation listed in a separate paper, equivalent to but not on form
    PTO/SB/08, and considered by the examiner will be printed on the
    patent if the list lends itself to easy capture of the necessary
    information by the Office printing contractor, i.e., each item of
    information is listed on a single line, the lines are at least
    double-spaced from each other, and the information is uniform in
    format for each listed...
    -   **Plain English:** In simpler terms, the result depends on the
        stated condition or exception: a citation listed in a separate
        paper, equivalent to but not on form PTO/SB/08, and considered
        by the examiner will be printed on the patent if the list lends
        itself to easy capture of the necessary information by the
        Office printing contractor, i.e., each item of information is
        listed on a single line, the lines are at least double-spaced
        from each...
-   For patents printed after January 1, 2001, citations from
    information disclosure statements that are printed on the face of
    the patent will be distinguished from citations cited by the
    examiner on a form PTO-892.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: for patents printed after
        January 1, 2001, citations from information disclosure
        statements that are printed on the face of the patent will be

    with an asterisk.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: the citations cited by the
        examiner on a form PTO-892 will be marked with an asterisk.
-   If an item of information is cited more than once in an IDS and on a
    form PTO-892, the citation of the item will be listed only once on
    the patent as a citation cited by the examiner.
    -   **Plain English:** In simpler terms, the result depends on the

        cited more than once in an IDS and on a form PTO-892, the
        citation of the item will be listed only once on the patent as a

## § 609.08 --- Electronic Processing of Information Disclosure Statement

    PTO/SB/08) submitted as part of an information disclosure statement

    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: the USPTO electronically
        processes the list of citations (e.g., form PTO/SB/08) submitted

    citations and electronically sign the IDS when reviewing the cited
    references.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: examiners are provided with

        sign the IDS when reviewing the cited references.

        determining whether a cited reference has been considered by the
        examiner.

    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: the electronically
