## § 1702 --- Restrictions on Current and Former Office Employees Regarding Patent Matters

    incapable, during the period of their appointments and for one year
    thereafter, of applying for a patent and of acquiring, directly or
    indirectly, except by inheritance or bequest, any patent or any
    right or interest in any patent, issued or to be issued by the
    Office.
    -   **Plain English:** The practical rule is: officers and employees
        of the Patent and Trademark Office must be incapable, during the
        period of their appointments and for one year after that, of
        applying for a patent and of acquiring, directly or indirectly,
        except by inheritance or bequest, any patent or any right or
        interest in any patent, issued or to be issued by the Office.
