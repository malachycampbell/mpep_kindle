## § 1605 --- Specification and Claim

        plant has been asexually reproduced.

## § 1611 --- Issue

    same procedure as for other applications (37 CFR 1.161), with the
    exception that where there are color drawings, the better one of the
    two judged, for example, by its sharpness or cleanliness is selected
    to be printed in the patent.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: the preparation of a plant

        there are color drawings, the better one of the two judged, for
