-   (G) An examiner seeking the transfer of a case may make a search,
        both of their own class and the class to which they think the
        case should be transferred, and the examiner in charge of the
        art unit should ensure the record includes the result of the
        search.

    -   **Plain English:** This means the rule allows, but does not
        necessarily require, the following: (G) An examiner seeking the
        transfer of a case may make a search, both of their own class
        and the class to which they think the case should be
        transferred, and the examiner in charge of the art unit should
        ensure the record includes the result of the search.
