*MPEP Ninth Edition, Revision 01.2024 (November 2024)*

## § 1901.01 --- Who Can Protest

-   Any member of the public, including private persons, corporate
    entities, and government agencies, may file a protest under 37 CFR
    1.291.
    -   **Plain English:** This means the rule allows, but does not
        necessarily require, the following: any member of the public,
        including private persons, corporate entities, and government
        agencies, may file a protest under 37 CFR 1.291.
-   A protest may be filed by an attorney or other representative on
    behalf of an unnamed real party in interest.
    -   **Plain English:** This means the rule allows, but does not
        necessarily require, the following: a protest may be filed by an
        attorney or other representative on behalf of an unnamed real
        party in interest.
-   In accordance with 37 CFR 1.291(b)(2), a statement must accompany
    the protest that it is the first protest submitted in the
    application by the real party in interest who is submitting the
    protest.
    -   **Plain English:** The practical rule is: under 37 CFR
        1.291(b)(2), a statement must accompany the protest that it is
        the first protest submitted in the application by the real party
        in interest who is submitting the protest.
-   The requirements of 37 CFR 1.291(c)(5) cannot be avoided by multiple
    protests submitted by different people representing the same real
    party in interest.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: the requirements of 37 CFR
        1.291(c)(5) cannot be avoided by multiple protests submitted by
        different people representing the same real party in interest.
-   A protestor may not know if a protest has already been filed (by
    another), and may have no way of checking (e.g., a non-reissue
    application for which the file wrapper is not publicly available).
    -   **Plain English:** This means the rule allows, but does not
        necessarily require, the following: a protestor may not know if
        a protest has already been filed (by another), and may have no

    -   **Plain English:** This means the rule allows, but does not

        problems may arise, and have to be resolved, does not preclude
        the Office from considering such evidence, nor does it mean that

-   Information in a protest should be set forth in the manner required

    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: information in a protest

-   While the forms in which evidence and/or information may be

        other information submitted; a concise explanation of the

## § 1901.04 --- When Can the Protest Be Submitted

-   As a practical matter, any protest should be submitted as soon as
    possible after the protestor becomes aware of the existence of the
    application to which the protest is to be directed.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: as a practical matter, any
        protest should be submitted as soon as possible after the
        protestor becomes aware of the existence of the application to
        which the protest is to be directed.
-   By submitting a protest early in the examination process, i.e.,
    before the Office acts on the application if possible, the protestor
    ensures that the protest will be of the most benefit to the Office
    in its examination of the application.
    -   **Plain English:** In simpler terms, the result depends on the
        stated condition or exception: by submitting a protest early in
        the examination process, i.e., before the Office acts on the
        application if possible, the protestor ensures that the protest
        will be of the most benefit to the Office in its examination of
        the application.
-   If, for some reason, the protest of the reissue application cannot
    be filed within the 2-month period provided by MPEP § 1441, the
    protest can be submitted at a later time, but the protestor must be
    aware that reissue applications are "special" and a later filed
    protest may be received after action by the examiner.
    -   **Plain English:** The practical rule is: if, for some reason,

    Gazette , and a delay of the examination until the extended period

    37 CFR 1.17(f).
    -   **Plain English:** In simpler terms, the result depends on the

## § 1901.06 --- Office Treatment of Protest

-   If a compliant protest is filed in a reissue application and the
    reissue application is related to a patent involved in a pending
    interference or derivation proceeding, such application should be
    referred to the Office of Patent Legal Administration before the
    examiner considers the protest and acts on the application.
    -   **Plain English:** In simpler terms, the result depends on the
        stated condition or exception: if a compliant protest is filed
        in a reissue application and the reissue application is related
        to a patent involved in a pending interference or derivation
        proceeding, that application should be referred to the Office of
        Patent Legal Administration before the examiner considers the
        protest and acts on the application.
-   If the Office's initial review reveals that the protest is ready for
    consideration during the examination, the examiner may nevertheless
    consider it desirable, or necessary, to obtain applicant's comments
    on the protest before further action.
    -   **Plain English:** This means the rule allows, but does not
        necessarily require, the following: if the Office's initial
        review reveals that the protest is ready for consideration
        during the examination, the examiner may nevertheless consider
        it desirable, or necessary, to obtain applicant's comments on
        the protest before further action.
-   In such situations, the examiner will offer applicant an opportunity
    to file comments within a set period, usually 1 month, unless
    circumstances warrant a longer period.
    -   **Plain English:** In simpler terms, the result depends on the

        will offer applicant an opportunity to file comments within a

## § 1901.07 --- Protestor Participation

-   37 CFR 1.291 does not permit protestor, or any other member of the
    public, to contact or receive information from the Office as to the
    disposition or status of the protest, or the application to which it
    is directed, or to participate in any Office proceedings relating to
    the protest.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: 37 CFR 1.291 does not
        permit protestor, or any other member of the public, to contact
        or receive information from the Office as to the disposition or
        status of the protest, or the application to which it is
        directed, or to participate in any Office proceedings relating
        to the protest.
-   The Office does not serve copies of Office actions or other
    documents mailed by the Office on protestors, and does not require
    applicants to serve copies of papers filed with the Office on
    protestors.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: the Office does not serve
        copies of Office actions or other documents mailed by the Office
        on protestors, and does not require applicants to serve copies
        of papers filed with the Office on protestors.
-   Furthermore, a protestor is not permitted to participate in
    interviews, appeal a decision by the examiner adverse to the
    protestor to the Patent Trial and Appeal Board, or participate in an
    appeal by applicant.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: furthermore, a protestor is
        not permitted to participate in interviews, appeal a decision by
        the examiner adverse to the protestor to the Patent Trial and
        Appeal Board, or participate in an appeal by applicant.
-   The disposition of the protest will be an ex parte matter between
    the Office and the applicant.
    -   **Plain English:** In plain English: the disposition of the
        protest will be an ex parte matter between the Office and the
        applicant.
-   Where protestor has access to an application, for example, a reissue
    application which is open to the public and may be inspected under
    37 CFR 1.11, the proceedings may thereby be monitored.
    -   **Plain English:** This means the rule allows, but does not
        necessarily require, the following: where protestor has access
        to an application, for example, a reissue application which is
        open to the public and may be inspected under 37 CFR 1.11, the
        proceedings may thereby be monitored.
